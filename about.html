About Miss Mhlongo

Where Maths Makes Sense. Building confidence, improving understanding and helping learners achieve their potential.

Our Story

Making Learning Make Sense

Mathematics With Miss Mhlongo is a tutoring initiative focused on helping learners develop a clearer understanding of their schoolwork.

We recognise that every learner is different. Some concepts take more time, some require additional practice, and some simply need to be explained in a different way.

Our aim is to make learning more approachable by breaking down difficult concepts into manageable steps and encouraging learners to keep progressing.

Our Mission

To support learners as they strengthen their academic skills, improve their understanding and build confidence in their abilities.

Our Approach

To encourage learning through clear explanations, guided examples, regular practice and patience.

Our Vision

To help learners approach challenging schoolwork with greater confidence and become more independent problem-solvers.

Our Philosophy

Every Step Forward Matters

Understanding is more valuable than simply memorising an answer. When learners understand how and why a method works, they can apply that knowledge to new questions.

We want learners to feel comfortable asking questions, learning from mistakes and practising until concepts become clearer.

01. Understand

Build a solid foundation by understanding the concept before moving forward.

02. Practise

Work through examples and questions to strengthen knowledge and skills.

03. Progress

Use practice and feedback to recognise improvements and identify areas that need more attention.
